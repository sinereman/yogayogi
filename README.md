## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. I cannot hear the Voice Coach or background music. What should I do?
Yoga Yogi is designed to work with your device's audio settings.
* **Check the Silent Switch:** Please ensure the silent switch on the side of your iPhone is **OFF** (no orange showing).
* **Volume:** Make sure your device volume is turned up.
* **Bluetooth:** Check if your device is connected to a Bluetooth speaker or headphones.

### 2. How does the 90-Day Plan work?
The app provides a structured daily flow. You start from Day 1 and progress through the journey. As you complete sessions, you unlock badges and progress to higher levels (Level 1, 2, and 3).

### 3. Can I use the app offline?
Yes, once the daily content is loaded, you can practice your flow. However, an internet connection is recommended for the best experience and to sync your progress.

### 4. How do I change my difficulty level?
You can select your preferred level (Level 1, 2, or 3) from the main dashboard or the settings menu. We recommend starting at Level 1 if you are a beginner.

### 5. How do I restore my purchases?
If you have purchased a premium subscription or lifetime access and reinstalled the app:
1.  Go to the **Settings** or **Paywall** screen.
2.  Tap on the **"Restore Purchases"** button.

## 🗑️ Account & Data Deletion

We value your privacy. If you wish to delete your user data or account:
1.  **In-App:** Go to **Settings > Delete Account** (if available in your version).
2.  **By Email:** Send a request to `batikankursat@outlook.com` with the subject "Delete My Data". We will process your request and delete your data within 30 days.
